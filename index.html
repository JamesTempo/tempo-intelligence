FAQ

Good questions.

What happens with emergencies?

Urgent calls, like a burst pipe or no heat, go straight to a real person on your team. The system never handles an emergency on its own.

What if a customer writes back?

Your team gets told right away, with the full message. You decide how to answer.

Does it work with my software?

We work with the phone line, calendar and booking tool you already use. We check this on the free call before you pay anything. We also offer custom builds.

Is it legal to text my customers?

Yes, when it's done right. Every message follows Canada's anti-spam law: it says who it's from and how to stop. All texts are in French first.
